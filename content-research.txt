About page
Our History (The Roots)
Lekker Organic Farm began in 2012 with a simple mission: to heal the soil and feed the soul. What started as a small family vegetable patch in Meyerton has grown into a leading local producer of organic plant produce and fresh dairy. We realized that the community was hungry for food that wasn't treated with harsh chemicals or travelled thousands of kilometres to reach the shelf.

Our Philosophy (The Heart)
We operate on the principle of Ubuntu—I am because we are. We believe that if we look after the earth (umhlaba), the earth will look after us. Our farming methods focus on:

Soil Health: Using natural compost and crop rotation instead of synthetic fertilizers.

Animal Welfare: Our dairy herd is small, grass-fed, and treated with the respect they deserve.

Sustainability: Reducing our carbon footprint by selling directly to our neighbours in Gauteng.

Our Mission
To provide the freshest, most nutrient-dense organic produce and dairy in the Vaal Triangle while educating our community on the importance of sustainable living.
