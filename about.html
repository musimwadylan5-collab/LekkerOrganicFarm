Our Story

Rooted in the heart of Meyerton, Gauteng, Lekker Organic Farm is more than just a place where things grow, it is where we cultivate a better way of living. Since our journey began in 2012 as a small family run venture, we have been on a mission to prove that you do not need chemicals to produce a world-class harvest.

What started on a modest plot has blossomed into a thriving agricultural hub. Today, our fields are home to vibrant vegetable crops, our pastures host happy free-range poultry, and our dairy unit produces the kind of freshness that makes you say "yo, this is the real deal."

Our Story

From day one, we have been about that plaas life. Over the last decade, we have expanded our reach from local weekend markets to the kitchens of top-tier restaurants and health-conscious households across the region. We have built our reputation on a simple foundation: honesty, hard work, and a deep respect for the umhlaba.

Our Mission

To provide high quality, organically grown produce while promoting sustainable farming practices that protect the environment and support community well being. We want to ensure that every baba, gogo, and outjie has access to food that is as healthy as it is mnandi.

Our Vision

To become a leading regional supplier of organic produce and a recognised advocate for environmentally responsible agriculture. We are working toward a South Africa where sustainable farming is not the exception it is the standard.

Why Our Produce is Kwaai

  • Zero Chemical Nonsense: We believe nature knows best. No synthetic pesticides, no weird fertilizers—just pure, organic goodness.
  • Sustainable to the Core: Our methods are designed to protect the Gauteng soil for generations to come. We give back more than we take.
  • Meyerton Fresh: Because we are local, our produce doesn't spend days in a truck. It gets to you with maximum spoed, locked in with nutrients and flavour.
  • The Spirit of Ubuntu: We aren't just farming for ourselves; we are farming for a healthier, stronger community.
