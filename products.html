Grown in Meyerton, Loved by the Earth

At Lekker Organic Farm, we focus on two things: nutrient-dense plants and pure, fresh dairy. No chemicals, no shortcuts—just mnandi food for your kombuis.

Organic Plant Produce

Our plant selection changes with the South African seasons. From crisp spinach and kale to sun-ripened tomatoes and hearty root vegetables, we bring the best of the umhlaba straight to your table.

Fresh Farm Dairy

Our dairy unit is small but mighty. We offer raw milk and handmade butter produced right here in Meyerton. It is creamy, natural, and free from synthetic hormones.
